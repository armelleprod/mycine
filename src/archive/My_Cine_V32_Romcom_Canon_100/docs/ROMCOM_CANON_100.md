# My Ciné Romcom Canon 100

This release activates a 100-title editorial Romcom library.

Rules:
- Alternatives must be Canon-approved Romcom titles.
- Current-year Tonight's Pick may be provisional, but must pass strict Comedy + Romance checks.
- Forrest Gump, Charade, Poor Things, PK, Dos tipos de cuidado and other false positives are explicitly excluded.
- Start Fresh preserves the previous batch in the exclusion list.
- Recent history is never bypassed for Romcom.
- Every displayed title must retain a live TMDB score of at least 7.5/10.
