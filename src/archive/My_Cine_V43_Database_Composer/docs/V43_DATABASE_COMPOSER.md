# V43 Full-Database Romcom Composer

V43 no longer walks through the Canon in sequential chunks.

It:
- builds an editorial shortlist from the entire unused database
- uses Canon tier, role, viewer fit, era and country before TMDB enrichment
- requires at least two Masterpiece/Essential titles per batch
- allows exactly one classic
- allows at most one East Asian title
- allows at most two titles per country and decade
- enforces Movies/TV format with no fallback exception
- preserves the session no-repeat ledger

There is no recovery mode that may violate the Rule of Seven.
